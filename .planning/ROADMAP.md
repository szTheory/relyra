# Roadmap: Relyra

## Overview

Relyra is a strict-by-default SAML 2.0 Service Provider library for Elixir/Phoenix. The v1.x arc is shipped through **v1.7 — Adoption Evidence Demo** (incl. post-milestone phases 57/57.1) and the non-protocol **v1.8 — Brand System & Identity** (Phases 58–63). **No active milestone** — future protocol scope is demand-gated; the next milestone starts with `/gsd:new-milestone`.
