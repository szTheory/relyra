<objective>
Freeze the Phase 08 public seam before the Ecto adapter lands: tighten the
resolver contract around a fully normalized runtime snapshot, document the
canonical `idp_certificates` field, and bound resolver failures to a
small `%Relyra.Error{}` taxonomy with precise safe details.
</objective>

<threat_model>
  <threat id="TM-08-01-CONTRACT-DRIFT" severity="high">
    If the public resolver seam stays map-generic and certificate naming
    remains split, the Ecto adapter can ship with duplicate normalization
    logic, vague diagnostics, and long-term `cert_chain` drift in runtime
    consumers.
  </threat>
  <mitigations>
    - Update `Relyra.ConnectionResolver` docs, callback intent, and
      invalid-adapter-result handling so the public contract points to a
      fully normalized `%Relyra.Connection{}` per D-05, D-08, D-09, and D-13.
    - Keep the top-level resolver taxonomy intentionally small per D-14
      and push specific causes into `details.reason`, `connection_id`,
      `operation`, and other redaction-safe fields per D-15 and D-16.
    - Update default/fixture adapters and login-facing tests toward
      canonical `idp_certificates`, while allowing `cert_chain` only as
      temporary compatibility glue per D-10 and D-11.
  </mitigations>
  <verification_commands>
    - `mix test test/relyra/connection_test.exs test/phoenix/login_controller_test.exs --warnings-as-errors`
    - `rg -n "idp_certificates|cert_chain|resolve_connection|details.reason|connection_unavailable|connection_invalid|resolver_misconfigured|resolver_failed" lib/relyra/connection_resolver.ex lib/relyra/connection_resolver/default.ex test/support/fake_connection_resolver.ex`
  </verification_commands>
</threat_model>
