<objective>
Publish `guides/troubleshooting.md` as the SAML Error Atom Decoder for every atom
`Relyra.Error` emits, and gate the document against the codebase with an automated
bidirectional drift-check test. Wire both surfaces into `mix.exs` (`extras:`) and the
`ci.docs` alias (presence guard + drift test).

This plan closes **DOCS-06** from ROADMAP Phase 40. The drift test is authored FIRST
(failing), then the guide is authored bucket-by-bucket until the test goes green. The
test moduledoc documents the variadic-helper rule from RESEARCH.md Pitfall 1.

Purpose: Operators encounter cryptic SAML atoms (`:digest_mismatch`,
`:doctype_forbidden`, `:replayed_assertion`, ...) in logs and need a single
authoritative decoder. Without a drift test, the decoder will rot the first time
someone adds a new atom in `lib/` without updating the guide. The bidirectional
drift assertion makes documentation a build-time contract, not a courtesy.

Output:
- `test/docs/troubleshooting_drift_test.exs` — one ExUnit module, one test, two
  assertions (missing-in-doc + stale-in-doc).

  four-field micro-block per atom.

  entries per D-17), append `"cmd test -f guides/troubleshooting.md"` to `ci.docs`

  AFTER both presence guards per D-19.
